# Research upgrade delivery order

September 8, 2026. This consolidates the current milestone and older blueprint engineering steps. Listed work is pending unless marked delivered.

1. **Delivered: shared research snapshot release.** DFS Daily and Simulation Lab resolve one immutable bundle through `/research/latest.json`. Public DK/ESPN collection, source-age readiness, failure retention and identity/exclusion diagnostics are implemented. A Mac-based task is active at 10:00 and 17:00 America/Chicago; it publishes changed validated data. No production Cron or D1 migration was added.
2. **Identity and schedule foundation.** Add an evidence-backed, effective-dated provider→GSIS review ledger; never auto-verify name matches. Normalize current season/week games and sportsbook events so prop lines and persisted matchup signals share authoritative game IDs. The current scoreboard validates the selected DFS slate but does not populate all live odds events into D1.
3. **Final actuals and calibration.** Implement the [calibration plan](calibration-plan.md), including final-stat ingestion, comparable active/limited samples, frozen forecasts, chronological fitting and held-out evaluation. Preserve corrections and historical line snapshots, including defined closing-line timing for prop evaluation.
4. **Role and independent forecast inputs.** Remove fixed-season assumptions throughout legacy ingestion; use dated current-season samples and recency weights. Add routes/snaps, practice reports, inactives, depth charts, projected starters and teammate availability. Segment comparable roles and redistribute usage explicitly. Current coordinator/play-caller/head-coach tenure and system changes should reset or downweight prior-system evidence. Team positional splits cannot substantiate shadow-corner or individual coverage claims; those require suitable licensed evidence.
5. **Transparent Dr Locks score and roster grading.** Keep component projections distinct, expose missingness, and implement the requested configurable player score. Grade legal rosters from joint draws and re-simulate suggested replacements. Do not equate a research score with a calibrated contest probability.
6. **Newsroom and linked scenarios.** Add structured sourced news and evidence-backed player/team/game impacts, with suggested temporary scenario changes and links from cards. No silent forecast override.
7. **Persisted production signals and specialized markets.** After schedule/identity correctness and quota review, persist compact versioned matchup model runs for browser reads. Fit and validate TD, alternate-line, long-reception, combo and defensive-player markets separately. The current salary refresh task does not refresh paid odds or persist board signals.
8. **Contest modeling.** Ownership, field simulation, top-10% probability and ROI optimization follow the above evidence gates. No contest entry or wagering automation.
