Player values PARTIAL · research heuristic

Rank within position using available salary efficiency, projection, simulated ceiling / breakout, and projected targets + carries. Missing components are excluded and weights rescaled. Matchup, weather, injury opportunity, ownership and production gap are not scored yet.

How Value Score works

Breakout = fraction of Monte Carlo draws above 3× salary / 1,000. This threshold is a configurable research assumption, not a calibrated tournament-winning requirement. P90 is an individual scoring percentile, not a top-10% contest finish.

Simulation Station

Uses the existing joint slate Monte Carlo engine. Scenario assumptions change opportunity; outputs are research estimates with uncalibrated variance/correlation. Weather, routes and red-zone controls are unavailable in this engine.
