Assumptions & sources

Four regulation quarters. No overtime, penalties, timeouts, player allocation, injury or weather adjustments. Automatic PATs, simplified fourth downs and kicks. The foundation uses configurable statistical assumptions; the empirical experiment fits league yardage histograms, conditional play calls and approximate pace using 2024 NFLverse play-by-play. Team inputs also use 2024 data.

2025 is held out for evaluation. The empirical yardage distributions do not account for the opponent or current roster. News and market lines do not change these simulations. Snap-gap pace estimates subtract an assumed six-second play duration.
