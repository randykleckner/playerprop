Assumptions & sources

Four regulation quarters. No overtime, penalties, timeouts or weather adjustments. V2-D uses official injury/practice evidence and ephemeral availability scenarios. V2 Base and Personnel now allocate offensive player statistics from simulated plays. Automatic PATs, simplified fourth downs and kicks. The foundation uses configurable statistical assumptions; the empirical experiment fits league yardage histograms, conditional play calls and approximate pace using recency-weighted NFLverse play-by-play. Current-season observations enter when available, with historical shrinkage; the data vintage above lists actual seasons.

The separate frozen 2024 profile is evaluated against 2025. That check does not validate the current profile. Legacy empirical distributions do not account for the opponent or current roster. The separately selected V2 Personnel engine applies bounded unit modifiers; V2 Base preserves the empirical control. News and market lines do not change these simulations. Snap-gap pace estimates subtract an assumed six-second play duration.
